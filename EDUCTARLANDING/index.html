Process

How Eductar works

Upload a PDF. Eductar handles the rest — no prompting, no manual formatting.

01

Upload Your PDF

Drop in any lecture note, textbook chapter, or slide deck. Eductar handles text-heavy PDFs and scanned pages equally.

02

AI Reads and Understands

A multi-model LLM chain processes each page — using vision models for diagrams and scanned content that text extraction misses.

03

Content is Restructured

Raw information is reorganised across Bloom's six cognitive levels — from basic recall up to evaluation and creation tasks.

04

Learn Progressively

Study through structured outputs delivered in sequence. Daily learning nudges are pushed directly to your device via Firebase.

Framework

Built on Bloom's Taxonomy

Every piece of content is mapped to one of six cognitive levels — so you always know what depth you are studying at.

Level 1

Remember

Recall key definitions, facts, and terminology directly from your notes. The foundation of all deeper understanding.

Level 2

Understand

Explain concepts in your own words, summarise ideas, and interpret what your notes are actually saying.

Level 3

Apply

Use knowledge in new situations — calculations, worked examples, and scenario-based questions drawn from your material.

Level 4

Analyse

Break down complex ideas, compare concepts, and identify relationships between topics across your lecture content.

Level 5

Evaluate

Critique arguments, judge the validity of claims, and reason about trade-offs presented in your notes.

Level 6

Create

Synthesise material into new ideas, design solutions, and produce original work grounded in what you have learned.

Features

Built for serious students

Every decision was made around how students actually learn — not how apps usually get built.

Multi-model LLM Chains

Automatic fallback across multiple language models via OpenRouter — if one fails, the next takes over without losing progress.

Vision Model Support

Per-page rendering catches vector diagrams and scanned content that text extraction alone would miss entirely.

Daily Learning Nudges

Firebase FCM delivers bite-sized content to your device on a schedule — APScheduler ensures consistent daily delivery.

Rate-limit Aware Processing

Chunked processing with sleep delays handles free-tier API limits gracefully — no crashes, no lost work mid-document.

FastAPI Backend

Production-grade REST API built with FastAPI, SQLAlchemy ORM, Pydantic validation, and PostgreSQL on Neon.

Any Subject, Any Level

Works across engineering, medicine, law, and humanities. Content-agnostic by design — if it is in the PDF, it gets structured.
